# DEGRE Mods for Sauce4Zwift

Professional widget collection for competitive Zwift racing and streaming.

**Author:** Luca De Gregorio  
**Version:** 3.7.0  
**License:** MIT

---

## Widgets Included

### 🔋 Power Bar
Dynamic power zone visualization with real-time feedback.

- Real-time power display with official zone colors (Z1-Z7)
- Stepped gradient with gap lines between zones
- Vertical or horizontal orientation
- Display W/kg or Watts with configurable scale
- Hide when not racing option
- Customizable zone thresholds and colors

### 🎯 Attack Radar
Detects attacks in your group with audio alerts and detailed attacker stats.

- Configurable detection thresholds: Watts and W/kg (AND/OR logic)
- Single attacker: shows name, power, w/kg, weight, position (ahead/behind)
- Multiple attackers: shows count, average stats, position breakdown
- Separate audio alerts for opponents, teammates, or mixed attacks
- Team race mode to distinguish teammates from opponents
- Active only in final kilometers (configurable distance)
- Preview mode for positioning

### 🏁 Finish Well
Final kilometers countdown with bell, sprint loop, and checkered flag.

- Distance countdown display (meters)
- Bell sound at configurable distance (default 1km)
- Sprint audio loop in final meters
- Checkered flag 🏁 and applause at finish
- Auto-detection of sprint efforts
- Customizable visibility distance

### 🎵 Player
Music player with NowPlay integration for streaming.

- Plays music from NowPlay playlists
- Album cover display (square layout)
- Play/Pause, Next, Shuffle controls
- Volume control with slider
- Track info and progress bar (optional)
- Copyright banner for streaming compliance
- Autoplay option

### 📻 Radio
NowPlay web radio inside Sauce, in sync with everyone listening on the website and on Discord.

- "Now playing" bar that fits any window shape (wide, square, tall): the text always fills the space
- One button: click to switch the radio on/off, mouse wheel for the volume
- Tap the station name to change station; cover and title stay updated even when the radio is off
- Stations (Zwift Time, All Songs, Luca's Favorite, Abyss Bloom, Pop)
- Thumbs up/down on the track on air
- Song requests: pick a track of the station, it plays right after the current one
- Autoplay when the widget opens or when an event starts
- Royalty-free music by Luca DEGRE, safe for streaming

### 💬 Chat Translate
The Zwift chat, translated into your language.

- Every message translated as it arrives; messages already in your language are left untouched
- Free Google translator, no setup; or your own Gemini key for a better grasp of Zwift slang
- Optional read-aloud of the translated messages (your team only, or everyone)
- Adjustable text size, follows the newest message automatically
- Read only: Sauce follows the race in Fan View, so it cannot send messages or see private ones

### 📜 Ticker
Customizable scrolling text overlay.

- Scrolling marquee text
- Configurable speed and direction
- Custom text, font size, and colors
- Visibility cycling options
- Perfect for sponsor messages or stream info

## Installation

1. Download the latest release ZIP
2. Extract to your SauceMods folder:
   - **Windows:** `C:\Users\<YourName>\Documents\SauceMods\`
   - **macOS/Linux:** `~/Documents/SauceMods/`
3. Restart Sauce for Zwift
4. Enable widgets from the Sauce menu

---

## Requirements

- Sauce for Zwift v1.0.0 or later
- Player and Radio stream from www.lucadegre.it (internet connection required)
- Chat Translate sends the chat messages to Google Translate (or to Gemini, if you choose it) to translate them
